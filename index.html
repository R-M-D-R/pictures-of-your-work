📸 How to Scan Your Work

In this course, you will complete your work on paper and upload it. After finishing your assignment, you must convert your work into a PDF file.

You may use:

  • an iPhone
  • an Android phone
  • a document scanning app
Important: Your pages should be:
  • clear and readable
  • right-side up
  • combined into ONE PDF file
Do NOT use your normal camera app to take pictures.
Instead, use a document scanning tool or scanning app. Scanning apps automatically:
  • combine pages into one PDF file
  • improve image clarity
  • crop pages correctly
  • make submissions easier to grade

📱 Using an iPhone

  1. Open Notes and create a new note.
  2. Tap the Camera button and choose Scan Documents.
  3. Place your paper inside the camera frame.
  4. If Auto mode is enabled, the phone will scan automatically. Otherwise, press the shutter button manually.
  5. Adjust the corners if needed, then tap Keep Scan.
  6. Add additional pages if necessary.
  7. Tap Save.
  8. Tap the three dots near the top-right corner.
  9. Select Send a Copy.
  10. Email the file to yourself as a PDF.

🤖 Using an Android Phone

  1. Open the Google Drive app.
  2. Tap the + button.
  3. Select Scan.
  4. Take a picture of your work.
  5. Use the editing tools if needed:
    • Crop to adjust the page
    • Re-scan if the image is blurry
    • Add to scan another page
  6. Tap Done when finished.
  7. Save and email the file to yourself as a PDF.

📄 Other Scanning Apps

If you prefer, you may also use a document scanning app. These apps can combine multiple pages into a single PDF file. Many of these apps are free.

Popular options include:

  • CamScanner
  • Adobe Scan
  • Microsoft Lens
Remember: Your final submission should be a single clear PDF file.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Uploading blurry pictures
  • Uploading multiple files (for example, one file per page)
  • Uploading sideways or upside-down pages
  • Cutting off part of the work
  • Submitting image files instead of a PDF
Clear uploads help your instructor grade your work quickly and accurately.
