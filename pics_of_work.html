Taking Pictures of Your Work

In this course, you will need to complete your work on your own paper. Once you've completed your work, you will need to get what you wrote down onto the computer.

To do this, you will use one of the following items:

  • an iPhone
  • an Android phone
  • a scanner

Choose the option below for instructions on how to complete this process.

Taking Pictures with an iPhone

  1. Open Notes and select a note or create a new one.
  2. Tap the Camera button, then tap Scan Documents.
  3. Place your document in view of the camera.
  4. If your device is in Auto mode, your document will automatically scan. If you need to manually capture a scan, tap the Shutter button or one of the Volume buttons. Then drag the corners to adjust the scan to fit the page, then tap Keep Scan.
  5. Tap Save or add additional scans to the document.
  6. Once you are finished taking all your pictures, select the three dots inside the circle at the top of the screen (next to Done).
  7. Scroll down to Send a Copy.
  8. Choose how you want to send it. I suggest you choose Mail and send it to your school email address.
    It will email it as a single PDF file. I suggest renaming it for your own sake.

Taking Pictures with an Android Phone

  1. Open the Google Drive app.
  2. In the bottom right, tap +.
  3. Tap Scan.
  4. Take a photo of the document you'd like to scan.
    • Adjust scan area: Tap Crop
    • Take photo again: Tap Re-scan current page
    • Scan another page: Tap Add
  5. To save the finished document, tap Done.
  6. When you are complete, email the file to yourself. Make sure it is in PDF file format.

Taking a Picture with a Scanner

If you have a scanner, you will need to follow the instructions according to your equipment's manufacturer. Keep in mind the following:

  • If you have multiple pages, you should scan all your pages in one session.
  • You should try to save all your pictures into a single file.
  • You should make that file in PDF format.
